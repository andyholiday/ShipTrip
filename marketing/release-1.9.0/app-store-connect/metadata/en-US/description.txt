Your personal cruise journal.

ShipTrip helps you plan every voyage, capture each port and turn your cruise history into a beautiful travel logbook. Routes, photos, excursions, expenses, journal entries and memories all live together in one private place.

CRUISE JOURNAL
• Create detailed entries for every cruise
• Save your ship, cabin, cruise line and travel dates
• Add personal notes and ratings
• Browse upcoming and completed voyages in your travel logbook

WIDGETS
• Keep your next cruise in view with a countdown on your Home or Lock Screen
• See the current port with time in port and the next stop at a glance
• Four sizes in the "Dynamic Instrument" look, from a small tile to a round Lock Screen widget
• Refreshes on its own whenever your cruise changes

JOURNAL ATLAS
• See your routes on an interactive world map
• Explore more than 1,800 ports worldwide
• Follow each voyage through an interactive stop timeline
• Quickly see the countries and ports you have visited

TRAVEL JOURNAL
• Capture notes, mood and photos for every port and sea day
• Add several entries per day, anchored right in your route
• Included in backup and export

PORT MOMENTS
• Capture port photos, arrival and departure times
• Keep excursions with the stop where they happened
• Build a visual record of every destination

SMART BOOKING IMPORT
• Paste text from a cruise booking confirmation
• Let AI extract the trip details for you
• Review the result before saving it

INSIGHTS AND EXPENSES
• Track cruises, days at sea, ports and countries
• Review your personal cruise history at a glance
• Record onboard and travel expenses
• Keep an eye on your budget

SHARE A CRUISE
• Send a single voyage as a .shiptrip file straight from the share sheet
• Recipients import it with one tap from iMessage, Mail or the Files app
• ShipTrip appears in the share sheet itself, ready to take in a received cruise file

PRIVATE SYNC AND BACKUP
• Sync travel data through your private iCloud account
• Optionally add individual stops or whole cruises to a calendar you choose
• Export your data as a ZIP archive and import it again when needed

ShipTrip supports English and German. Your travel data is stored locally and, when enabled, synchronized through the private iCloud/CloudKit area of your Apple account. ShipTrip does not operate its own servers for your journal data.
