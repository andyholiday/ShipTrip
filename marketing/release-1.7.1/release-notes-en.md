# ShipTrip 1.7.1 (Build 24) — English Release Copy

## TestFlight: What to Test

Maintenance release. Focus areas are reminders and editing ports.

- **Reminders**: Only one notification per cruise and type — even after saving
  repeatedly and after an iCloud sync. Please edit a cruise several times and
  check for duplicates.
- **Reminder settings**: After changing "Reminders on", "Days before" or
  "Departure reminder", restart the app once — the settings now also apply to
  cruises that were saved or imported earlier.
- **Editing a port**: For a port that is not in the catalog, set coordinates,
  then change another field and save. The pin must stay on the map.
- **Count texts**: "1 cruise" instead of "1 cruises", "Tomorrow" instead of
  "In 1 days" — in the cruise list, hero card, statistics, map, cruise detail,
  deals and settings, in German and English.
- **New notices**: Notice about the transfer to Google Gemini in the AI capture
  sheet; privacy and support links in the info section of the settings.

Known limitation: the count text "1 Kalendereinträge …" after a calendar change
still has no plural form.

## App Store: What's New

This update is about everyday reliability.

- Reminders arrive only once — even after editing and iCloud sync.
- Changed reminder settings now also apply to cruises saved or imported earlier.
- Custom port coordinates are preserved when editing.
- Count texts use proper plural forms in German and English.
- The AI capture sheet states that text is sent to Google Gemini for analysis.
- Settings now link to the privacy policy and support.
